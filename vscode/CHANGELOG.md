# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- The full ThirdNote library, reader, rich editor, board and flashcard review, the same as the browser extension.
- Edit notes like a document, with headings, lists, links, code blocks and images.
- Export to a real PDF file, or to Markdown.
- Right-click menu: ThirdNote > Highlight Yellow, Red, Green or Blue, with a note, as a snippet, and more.
- Your notes from 1.0 move over on their own.
