Features

Everything you need to remember

Highlight in four colours

Select text and press 1 to 4. Key point, Important, Example, Idea, or names you choose.

Save chats that keep growing

Press Save chat once. New messages are added automatically while you keep chatting.

Capture any part of the screen

Grab a video frame, a chart or a post. On YouTube the timestamp is saved with it.

One collection at a time

Switch on Collecting and every save, from any site, lands in the collection you are working on.

Review as flashcards

Your own questions become the prompts. Recall first, reveal, and mark what to see again.

Export anytime

Markdown or PDF, with highlights kept. Light and dark themes, in English and Bangla.

Highlight the answers that matter

A small toolbar appears under your selection. Pick a colour, add a note if you like, done. Highlights come back every time you open the page.

Everything worth remembering, in one place

The side panel keeps your collections, groups and highlights next to whatever you are reading. Search across all of it.

Remember anything

Review mode shows one card at a time. Your question on the front, your highlight on the back. Keyboard friendly: Space, 1, 2.

Private by design

ThirdNote has no servers and no accounts. Everything you save lives in your own browser, and you can download a full backup whenever you want.

Read the privacy policy
  • Stored only on your device
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads
  • Reads a chat only when you save it
